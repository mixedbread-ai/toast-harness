# Accessories and services price list (2025)

All prices in EUR, excluding VAT, valid alongside the Procurement FAQ. Accessories fit the Kestrel K200 and K400 flow sensors and the Osprey O-10 data logger unless noted.

Transducer pairs are ordered by pipe range: TP-25 for 25 to 100 mm is EUR 240, TP-100 for 100 to 400 mm is EUR 310, and TP-400 for 400 to 600 mm is EUR 460. Acoustic coupling gel CG-5 is EUR 18 per tube or EUR 180 for a 12-pack; the high-temperature variant CG-5H, required above 90 degrees C, is EUR 24 per tube.

Mounting hardware: the MK-40 stainless strap kit for pipes up to 200 mm is EUR 55, the MK-41 chain kit for larger pipes is EUR 85, and the WS-1 weather shield for outdoor installations is EUR 120. The AN-2 external antenna kit for the Osprey O-10 in underground pits is EUR 75.

Services: factory calibration in Parnu is EUR 260 per unit at the recommended interval, and an on-site calibration visit is EUR 950 per day plus travel; Gold-tier contracts include one on-site visit per year. Firmware upgrades are free for the lifetime of the product.

Communication options such as the ETH-1 Ethernet module for the K400 are listed with the sensor pricing in the Procurement FAQ, not here.
