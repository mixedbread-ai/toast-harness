# Meridian Instruments OÜ

Meridian Instruments is a fictional industrial sensor maker founded in 2011 in Tallinn, Estonia. The company designs and builds ultrasonic flow measurement equipment for water utilities, district heating networks and process industries. Headquarters and engineering are in Tallinn; manufacturing and calibration are in Pärnu.

The product range has two families. Kestrel is the line of clamp-on ultrasonic flow sensors, currently the K200 for small pipes and the K400 for the general range. Osprey is the line of battery-powered data loggers, currently the O-10, which records Kestrel readings and forwards them over cellular networks.

Meridian employs about 180 people. The chief executive is Liis Tamm; the head of customer support is Mart Kask. Sales run through regional distributors in the Nordics, the Baltics and Germany, with direct key accounts for utilities.
