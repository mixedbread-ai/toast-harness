# Kestrel K200 clamp-on ultrasonic flow sensor

The K200 is the small-pipe member of the Kestrel family, covering pipe diameters from 15 mm to 100 mm with an accuracy of plus or minus 2.0 percent of reading above 0.3 m/s. The part number is KS-200-A; the operating range is -20 to 80 degrees C, and there is no high-temperature version.

Power is 12 to 24 V DC at 4 W. The housing is IP66 and ships with a fixed transducer pair sized for the ordered pipe range. The recommended calibration interval is 24 months.

Communication is Modbus RTU over RS-485 only. The K200 has no expansion port, so the ETH-1 Ethernet module does not fit and Modbus TCP is not available on the K200; installations that need Modbus TCP should use the K400 instead. The K200 runs the same Kestrel firmware line as the K400 and is field-upgradeable through the service port.
