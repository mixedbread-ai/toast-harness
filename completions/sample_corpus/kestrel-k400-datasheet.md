# Kestrel K400 clamp-on ultrasonic flow sensor

The K400 measures liquid flow from outside the pipe using transit-time ultrasound, so no pipe cutting is needed. It covers pipe diameters from 25 mm to 600 mm with an accuracy of ±1.5% of reading above 0.3 m/s. The standard version carries part number KS-400-A and operates from -20 °C to 80 °C; the high-temperature version, part number KS-400-H, extends the range to 150 °C for district heating.

Power is 24 V DC at 6 W. The sensor is IP67 and ships with a transducer pair sized for the ordered pipe range. The recommended calibration interval is 18 months; calibration is performed at the Pärnu facility or by a certified distributor.

Communication is Modbus RTU over RS-485 as standard. Modbus TCP is available with the ETH-1 Ethernet module (part number ET-001) and requires Kestrel firmware 3.2 or later. Firmware is field-upgradeable through the service port with the Meridian Service Tool.
