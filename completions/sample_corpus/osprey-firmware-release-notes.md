# Osprey firmware release notes

## Osprey firmware 2.4 (released 2025-01-20)

Adds NB-IoT fallback when the LTE-M connection is lost, and fixes ticket MI-1240: duplicate uploads after a signal loss longer than 24 hours.

## Osprey firmware 2.3 (released 2024-08-02)

Raises the polling limit from two Kestrel sensors to four and adds a configurable 5-minute logging interval for commissioning.

## Osprey firmware 2.2 (released 2024-03-15)

Introduces over-the-air updates. Loggers on Osprey firmware 2.0 or 2.1 must be updated to 2.2 over a service-port cable with the Meridian Service Tool before over-the-air updates become available.
