# Osprey O-10 battery-powered data logger

The O-10 records readings from up to four Kestrel flow sensors over Modbus RTU on RS-485 and forwards them to the Meridian cloud over LTE-M or NB-IoT. The part number is OS-10-A.

Power comes from two D-cell lithium batteries with a five-year life at a 15-minute logging interval. The housing is IP68 and rated for flooded chambers; for underground pits with poor coverage, the AN-2 external antenna kit is available.

The O-10 runs Osprey firmware, a separate line from the Kestrel sensor firmware, and updates over the air once on version 2.2 or later. The logger polls sensors over RS-485 only; it does not support Modbus TCP and has no Ethernet option.
