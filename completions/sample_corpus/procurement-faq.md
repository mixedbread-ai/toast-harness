# Procurement FAQ

**How is the K400 priced?** The list price of the KS-400-A is EUR 1,850 per unit and of the KS-400-H EUR 2,300. Orders of 10 or more units of one part number receive a 12% volume discount. The ETH-1 Ethernet module (ET-001) is EUR 190.

**What are the lead times?** Standard K400 units ship within 3 weeks of order confirmation; the high-temperature KS-400-H ships within 6 weeks. Transducer pairs for pipes above 400 mm add one week.

**What are the payment terms?** Net 30 days from invoice for approved accounts; new customers prepay the first order. Quotes are valid for 60 days and are issued by the regional distributor or, for utilities, by Meridian's key account team.
