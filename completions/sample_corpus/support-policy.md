# Support and warranty policy

Kestrel flow sensors carry a 36-month warranty from the date of shipment; Osprey data loggers carry 24 months. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship, including transducer housings, but not damage from installation outside the specified temperature range. Firmware updates are free for the lifetime of the product.

Returns go through the RMA process: open a case in the support portal, and an RMA number is issued within 2 business days. Standard-tier customers ship the unit to Pärnu and receive a repaired or replacement unit within 15 business days. Gold-tier customers receive an advance replacement shipped the next business day.

Support tiers: Standard support is included with every purchase and answers by email within 2 business days. Gold support is an annual contract with phone support, a 4-hour response target, advance replacement, and an annual on-site calibration visit.
